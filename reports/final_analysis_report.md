## 4. Phase 4: Sensitivity Analysis

Sensitivity analysis tests how changes in key variables impact 2027 forecast outcomes. This is critical for understanding downside risks and validating forecast robustness.

### 4.1 Interest Rate Sensitivity (+1% Increase)

**Impact:** Additional interest expense of approximately $26.0M on FY2024 debt levels
- **After-tax impact:** -$19.5M on PAT (assuming 25% tax rate)
- **Percentage impact:** -10.8% reduction in 2027 PAT
- **Mitigation:** Fixed-rate debt locks in current rates; natural offset from higher deposit rates

### 4.2 Demand Shock Sensitivity (-5% TTV Reduction)

**Impact:** A 5% reduction in TTV would cascade through the model:
- **Revenue impact:** -$169M (-5.0%)
- **EBITDA impact:** -$37M (-5.9% due to operating leverage)
- **PAT impact:** -$11M (-6.1%)
- **Mitigation:** Geographic diversification, flexible cost structure, dynamic pricing

### 4.3 Currency Volatility (+5% AUD Depreciation)

**Impact:** Mixed effects on FLT's earnings:
- **Positive:** Increased inbound tourism revenue (+3-4% to EBITDA)
- **Negative:** Increased cost of international operations (+1-2% cost increase)
- **Net impact:** Approximately +1-2% to EBITDA
- **Mitigation:** Natural hedges from revenue/cost matching; potential formal hedging programs

### 4.4 Labor Cost Inflation (+3% Annual Increase)

**Impact:** Labor represents approximately 25% of revenue for FLT:
- **Additional labor costs:** $25.4M annually
- **EBITDA impact:** -$25.4M (-4.1% reduction)
- **Mitigation:** Automation, outsourcing, productivity improvements, pricing power

2. **Stable Macroeconomic Environment:** Assumes moderate inflation (2-3%), stable interest rates, no major recession
